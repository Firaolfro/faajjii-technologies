# FAAJJI TECHNOLOGIES: CLIENT DEMONSTRATION & PITCH SCRIPT

> **Goal:** Walk the client through the working platform in 7 minutes, demonstrate complete execution of their business proposal, and secure the contract for the ongoing project.

---

## 1. Opening Pitch (30 Seconds)

> *"Good morning / afternoon. Based on your strategic business proposal, I have architected and built the working digital foundation for **Faajji Technologies**.*
>
> *Rather than giving you slide decks or static designs, you can interact with a live, responsive platform uniting all three business divisions: **Software Services**, **Hardware Online Commerce**, and **EthioGatewayJobs**, all managed through a unified **Executive Operations Dashboard**.*
>
> *Let me walk you through how it works."*

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Live Walkthrough

### Step 1: Brand & Mobile Portal (Home)
1. **Show the Mobile Layout**: Press `F12` on your browser or show it on your mobile device.
2. Point out:
   - The custom **Faajji Orbit & Circuit 3D logos**.
   - The official company motto: *"Innovation • Technology • Opportunity • Digital Ethiopia"*.
   - Click **"Get Started"** or **"Login"** to demonstrate the exact authentication screen from their design mockup.
   - Point out the **Persistent Bottom Navigation** featuring the custom Ethiopian Lion emblem for Digital Ethiopia.

### Step 2: Division 1 — Enterprise Software & Live ERP Suite (Services)
1. Click the **"Software & Services"** tab.
2. Show the 5 core capability pillars:
   - Web applications, Mobile apps, AI automation, and IT support.
3. Scroll to **Business Management Systems** and click **"Launch Interactive ERP Demo"**:
   - **Tab 1: Inventory & Warehousing**: Show real-time SKU stock levels across Addis Ababa depots (Bole, Mercato, Kality) and add a test stock item.
   - **Tab 2: Invoicing & Ethiopian Tax**: Enter an organization name and click **"Generate & Print Tax Invoice"** to show a formal 15% VAT invoice compliant with Ethiopian standards.
   - **Tab 3: HR & Attendance**: Show employee attendance tracking and salary payroll.
   - **Tab 4: Hospitality & School**: Show room availability and student academic administration.
4. Close the modal and show the **Request for Proposal (RFP) Quote Form**:
   - Fill in a sample inquiry (e.g. *Bole Hospital - School / Hotel Management System - 150,000 ETB*), and hit submit!

### Step 3: Division 2 — Hardware Online Sales & Delivery (Hardware)
1. Click the **"Hardware Store"** tab.
2. Highlight:
   - Authentic Laptops, Cisco Switches, and HP Printers priced in **ETB** with warranty tags.
   - Fast category filtering (*Laptops, Networking, Printers, Peripherals*).
3. Click **"Add to Cart"** on a Dell Latitude laptop and Cisco switch.
4. Open the cart drawer, click **"Proceed to Delivery"**:
   - Select an Addis Ababa sub-city (*e.g., Bole, Kirkos, Yeka*).
   - Select payment method (*Chapa Telebirr/CBE or Cash on Delivery*).
   - Click **"Complete Order"** &rarr; An official reference code is generated (*e.g., FJ-948211*).

### Step 4: Division 3 — EthioGatewayJobs & AI Coach (Digital Ethiopia)
1. Click the **"Digital Ethiopia"** tab.
2. Show the **Verified Openings** list with entry-level and internship tags for fresh Ethiopian graduates.
3. Click the **"AI Interview Coach"** sub-tab:
   - Select *Junior Full Stack Developer* or *IT Support*.
   - Click **"Start AI Practice Session"**.
   - Type a response to the question and click **"Submit Answer for AI Scoring"**.
   - *Highlight to the client:* The AI scores the response out of 100, provides feedback, and delivers an official **Model Answer**!
4. Click the **"ATS CV Builder"** sub-tab:
   - Show how students can enter their credentials, click **"Download ATS PDF"**, and immediately generate a clean, professional ATS resume.

### Step 5: The Showstopper — Central Operations Dashboard (Admin)
1. Click the **"Admin"** badge in the top navigation bar.
2. Show the client:
   - **Gross Sales Revenue (ETB)**: Automatically aggregated from hardware sales.
   - **Orders Pipeline**: The order you placed in Step 3 is already there! Change its status from *Pending &rarr; Processing &rarr; Out for Delivery*.
   - **Client Inquiries**: The software quote you submitted in Step 2 is sitting in the CRM awaiting a contract.
   - **Job Applications**: View all applicants from the EthioGateway talent pool.
